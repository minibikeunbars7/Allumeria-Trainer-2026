## 🎮 About the Game

Allumeria is a voxel sandbox adventure RPG developed and published by Infinimelon. It entered Steam Early Access on August 28, 2026. The game combines exploration, survival crafting, base building, dungeon crawling, boss battles, procedural worlds, and online co-op.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Allumeria**. This trainer enhances Allumeria's exploration, crafting, building, and combat systems with optional quality-of-life enhancements. It can protect health and stamina, reduce environmental risks, simplify resource gathering, and provide greater control during dungeon runs and boss encounters.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Allumeria freely, experiment with advanced building tools, speed up crafting and resource collection, test weapons and trinkets, or reduce the difficulty of dungeons and boss battles.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Allumeria uses first-person movement with WASD, jumping, sprinting, climbing, gliding, and other abilities provided by trinkets. Infinite Stamina supports extended sprinting, climbing, and gliding, while No Fall Damage protects against drops in caves, dungeons, and tall structures. Movement options are best used during exploration or construction rather than during online co-op sessions.

### Tools & Inventory

Press E to open the inventory and crafting panel. The inventory stores collected blocks, ores, weapons, tools, trinkets, ammunition, and consumables. Unlimited Items and Easy Crafting reduce the need to repeatedly gather materials, while Unlimited Ammo supports bows, crossbows, explosive arrows, and other ranged equipment. Activate inventory options before opening the crafting interface.

### World & Environment

Allumeria features procedurally generated voxel worlds with multiple biomes, caves, structures, resources, dungeons, and destructible terrain. Fast Mining and One-Hit Mining accelerate gathering and excavation. Fast Building is useful for shaping blocks, creating stairs or fences, placing RGB lighting, and painting construction materials with the game's building tools.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat includes swords, crossbows, bows, bombs, explosive arrows, boomerangs, shuriken launchers, frying pans, snowball cannons, fart guns, and other weapons. Infinite Health protects against enemies and explosions, while Maximum Damage and No Weapon Cooldown make encounters faster. Use the combat options for dungeon raids and boss battles, then disable them when returning to normal progression.
